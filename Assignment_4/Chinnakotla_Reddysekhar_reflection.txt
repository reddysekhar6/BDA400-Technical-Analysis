Assignment 4 Reflection

The AI suggestions were most useful when they helped organize the cleaning pipeline into separate, testable steps. The important correction was adapting the generic assignment prompt to the actual supplied dataset. The file did not contain CustomerID, Region, or Revenue fields; instead, it contained OrderID, Product, Quantity, Price, and Customer. I therefore verified the real columns before applying any transformation. The verification step also showed why AI-generated code should not be accepted without checking the data: missing Product and Quantity values, duplicate rows, non-positive quantities, and the extreme quantity value of 1000 required dataset-specific decisions. The final checks confirmed that the cleaned data contained no missing values or duplicate rows and no non-positive quantities. A stronger prompt in a future task would provide the exact column names, data types, and a small sample of rows before asking for code. This would reduce the chance of receiving a technically valid solution that does not match the actual dataset.
